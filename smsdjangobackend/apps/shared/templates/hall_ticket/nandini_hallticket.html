General / Exam instructions for students

  1. Check the exam Timetable correctly.
  2. Students must bring the 'Admit Card' and show it to the invigilator on duty and should preserve for future requirements.
  3. Without 'Admit card' you may not be allowed to examination hall.
  4. Mobile phones, calculators, digital watch or any other electronic device are NOT ALLOWED.
  5. Arrive at the school at least 30 minutes before the beginning of the examination.
  6. Students are banned from entering the exam hall 15 minutes after the written examination starts.
  7. Ensure that you use the washroom before arriving for the exam as you will not be permitted to leave during the first hour of the examination.
  8. Normally you are required to answer / questions writing blue or blank ink; make sure you bring some spare pens with you.
